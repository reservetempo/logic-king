Lords & Logicians

The Book of Characters

Play the prototype

In this realm every thought travels on foot. Signals are torchbearers, peasants who walk the roads between gates, each carrying a single torch: a lit torch is 1, a dark one is 0. A gate takes the torchbearers who arrive and sends one torchbearer onward, lit or dark, according to the nature of whoever keeps it.

  • Peasants carry Torchbearers never change a torch on the road. They only walk.
  • Monks combine Logician monks weigh two torches and send one on.
  • Lords negate A lord overturns what reaches him. The greater lords keep a monk at court and overturn the monk.

Every silhouette echoes its gate symbol, and every lord wears a small ring (a ruff, a brooch, a clasp): the bubble of negation.

The signal

Hob Wickfoot

Torchbearer of the Low Road, and all his kin

Class
Peasant · carries
Carries
One torch, lit (1) or dark (0)

Personality

Cheerful, dutiful and wholly incurious. A torchbearer never lights or snuffs a torch on the road: he carries what he is handed to the next gate and waits to be told. Gossips in queues, waves at other torchbearers, and flinches whenever Lord Gainsay looks up.

Look

Small and round, with short legs and a bobbing walk so a crowd reads at a glance. Undyed wool tunic, patched hood, rope belt, bound feet. The torch rides high on a long pole so its state shows from across the map: lit, it throws a warm halo; dark, it is a charred wick with a thread of smoke. Hood colors (madder red, woad blue, weld yellow) tell individuals apart.

Gate · NOT

Lord Gainsay

Baron of Contrary Keep

Class
Lord · negates
Torches
One in, one out

The rule

Bring me fire and I snuff it. Bring me darkness and I light it, out of pure spite. Whatever you carry in, you carry the opposite out.

Truth table

0 dark1 lit
1 lit0 dark

Personality

Contrary to the bone: he says no before you finish asking, and yes if you ask for no. He never schemes and never makes exceptions, which makes the most spiteful man in the realm also its most reliable. Smirks whenever a lit torch walks up his road.

Look

Tall and gaunt, his black cloak falling from the shoulders in a hard triangle, with a round white ruff at the throat: the NOT symbol worn as a costume. Pale face, pointed beard, a long snuffer in one hand and a flint in the other. Colors: black, rubric-red lining, cold silver.

Gate · AND

Brother Bothwell

Prior of the Abbey of Concord

Class
Monk · combines
Torches
Two in, one out

The rule

Only when both of you arrive lit will I light the torch that leaves. One dark torch is dissent, and dissent means no.

Truth table

Personality

Patient, exacting and a little pedantic. He will not be hurried, and a majority of one does not move him; he counts both arrivals twice before he nods. Argues amiably with Brother Orrin at chapter every morning.

Look

Round-backed and solid: a brown habit and heavy cowl give him the flat-fronted, rounded D of the AND symbol. Tonsure, spectacles, a rope girdle tied in two knots, a ledger under one arm. Colors: habit brown, cream, a touch of gold leaf on the ledger.

Gate · OR

Brother Orrin

Almoner of Eithermere Priory

Class
Monk · combines
Torches
Two in, one out

The rule

If even one of you arrives lit, the torch that leaves is lit. Only two dark torches send a dark one on.

Truth table

0 dark0 dark0 dark
0 dark1 lit1 lit

Personality

Warm, generous and easily pleased: one flame is reason enough to celebrate. He hums while he works and is forever giving away his supper. Thinks Brother Bothwell far too strict, and tells him so, kindly.

Look

Lanky and light on his feet; a pointed cowl and a habit that sweeps back in a curve give him the shield-like outline of the OR symbol. Grey habit, lapis-blue scapular, an alms bag, and a small lantern at his belt that is always lit. Colors: dove grey, lapis, candle yellow.

Gate · NAND

Count Nandric

Count of Neverboth

Class
Lord · negates, with a monk of Concord (AND) at court
Torches
Two in, one out

The rule

My monk weighs the torches as the Abbey of Concord teaches; then I overturn him. Two lit torches leave dark. Anything less leaves lit.

Truth table

NAND: output for each pair of input torches
In AIn BOut
0 dark0 dark1 lit
0 dark1 lit1 lit
1 lit0 dark1 lit
1 lit1 lit0 dark

Personality

Suspicious of agreement: when everyone concurs he smells a plot and overturns it. In every other case he is easygoing and open-handed, so most torches leave his hall lit. Boasts that his house alone could govern the realm without any other gate, and, irritatingly, he is right.

Look

Stout, in a rounded surcoat that echoes the AND monk beside him, with a large gold ring brooch at the breast: the bubble of negation. A small brown-robed Concord monk stands at his elbow with an open ledger, so the sprite is a pair, lord in front and monk half a step behind. Colors: wine red, gold trim, habit brown.

Gate · NOR

Lady Norwenna

Margravine of Neithermoor

Class
Lord · negates, with a monk of Eithermere (OR) at court
Torches
Two in, one out

The rule

My almoner would light the way if either torch were lit. I forbid it. The torch that leaves burns only when both arrive dark.

Truth table

0 dark0 dark1 lit
0 dark1 lit0 dark
1 lit0 dark0 dark
1 lit1 lit0 dark

Personality

Cold, courteous and austere, a lover of the long night. She trusts darkness and takes any flame as a provocation, so only two dark torches please her. Like Count Nandric she could rule the whole realm with her house alone; unlike him, she would never say so aloud.

Look

Tall and still, in a midnight-blue gown whose curved, pointed train echoes the OR shape, with a round silver clasp at her collar. A grey Eithermere monk follows her carrying a lantern she keeps dark. Colors: midnight blue, silver, lapis, one rubric-red glove.
