## Shared context (include in every subagent prompt)

- Static site, **no build step**, published by GitHub Pages from `main` (repo root) at https://reservetempo.github.io/logic-king/ (repo `reservetempo/logic-king`, public). The game is at the root, the design doc at `design/`. The site is served under `/logic-king/`, so **use relative paths only** (`style.css`, `main.js`, `../`), never `/...`. `.nojekyll` is there so Pages serves files as is. Moved here from `dominic_my_website` (`public/gates/`), whose `/gates` and `/gates/play` now redirect here.
- `design/` is the design doc "Lords & Logicians" for a 2.5D game where logic gates are medieval people. **World rule:** signals are torchbearers (messengers) walking the roads between gates. **Lit torch = 1, dark = 0.** The doc's cast: **Hob Wickfoot**, the torchbearer (small round peasant, patched red hood, torch on a long pole), and **Lord Gainsay**, Baron of Contrary Keep, the **NOT** gate (tall and gaunt, black cloak falling in a hard triangle, round white ruff at the throat, so he wears the NOT symbol; pale face, pointed beard, a long snuffer in one hand and a flint in the other; colors black, rubric-red lining, cold silver). He snuffs a lit torch and, out of spite, lights a dark one. Read their sheets (`id="torchbearer"` and `id="not"` in `design/index.html`) with targeted greps, not whole-file reads. Portraits are inline SVG; edit the markup directly.
- **The game** (root): `index.html`, `style.css`, and ES modules `main.js` (entry: renderer, scene, loop, wiring), `map.js` (board data and tiles), `pieces.js` (characters, torches, beacon), `run.js` (the walk and the gate's moment). The user's choices: Three.js, low-poly chess-piece characters, "pick the torch, then send", a beacon at the road's end. Links: "Design notes" (`.doc-link`, end of the kicker line) → `design/`; the doc's "Play the prototype" (`.play-link`, under the subtitle) → `../`.
  - Three.js loaded with an import map pinned to `three@0.170.0` on jsDelivr: `"three": "https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/three@0.170.0/build/three.module.min.js"` and, only if an addon is needed, `"three/addons/": "https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/three@0.170.0/examples/jsm/"`. No npm dependencies.
  - **2.5D view:** fixed `OrthographicCamera` from due south (azimuth 0°, 45° down) looking north at the board centre, so the map text reads like the screen (right = east, up = north) and the road runs from the top of the screen to the bottom. Refit on resize so the whole board fills the canvas at any size. On screen the board is about 0.61 as wide as it is tall (the shape of a portrait phone stage). No orbit controls.
  - **Look:** dusk/night so torches matter: a cool moonlight directional light (soft shadows) plus a low hemisphere light, a deep night-blue background (`#1f2a4d`-ish). Colors copied from the palette tokens in `design/style.css` (`--flame-core`, `--flame`, `--flame-edge`, `--rubric`, `--gold`, `--lapis`, `--skin`, `--wool`, `--night`, `--silver`, etc.), not imported, so the two pages can drift. Page chrome (title, goal line, controls) in the doc's manuscript style: Google Fonts Cinzel (headings) and EB Garamond (text), parchment colors, with darker vellum under `prefers-color-scheme: dark`. The 3D scene stays dusk in both modes. `<title>` "Lords & Logicians · Prototype". Desktop keeps the framed layout (masthead, gold-framed stage, controls under it, 16px side gutter). **Phones** (`@media (max-width: 40rem), (max-height: 32rem)`, since 018db5f): the stage fills the viewport edge to edge (`100dvh`, no frame, no page scroll, `viewport-fit=cover` with safe-area padding). A slim translucent parchment bar (dark vellum in dark mode) floats over the bottom of the board with the goal "Light the beacon", the Lit/Dark toggle and Send, 36px tall. Upright it is about 65px on two rows (the goal on its own line in the gold rule); sideways it is one row (about 53px). The masthead, the "Hob's torch" legend and Reset are hidden (the `h1` and legend stay for screen readers; `#reset` stays in the DOM). `#readout` and `#status` float as a two-line caption just above the bar. Any window shorter than 32rem gets the phone layout too. Safe-area insets are untested on a real notched phone.
  - **Board** (in `map.js`, as data so later levels or road-building are easy). Rows of characters: `.` grass, `#` road, `S` start (Hob's village end), `N` Lord Gainsay's tile (a road tile with his small gatehouse; he stands beside the road), `B` beacon tower (road's end), `t` decor (tree or rock). Keep one unbroken road S → N → B with bends. Since f53ec19 the board is tall (6 × 13), used at every screen size; B is entered from the side so the tower stands beside the road's end:
    ```
    .t.S..
    ...#.t
    .###..
    .#..t.
    .#....
    .N##.t
    t..#..
    .t.#..
    ...##.
    .t..#.
    ...B#.
    t....t
    ..t...
    ```
    Tiles are separate slightly bevelled blocks with small gaps between them (grass tops, earthy sides). The road is a packed-earth strip inset on the tile top, shaped straight, corner or end from its road neighbours.
  - **Play:** the player picks Hob's torch, **Lit (1)** or **Dark (0)** (the torch at the start updates at once), and presses **Send**. Hob walks the road to Lord Gainsay, stops on his tile, the lord snuffs a lit torch or lights a dark one, and **Hob himself continues** with the opposite torch to the beacon. The beacon lights only if he arrives lit. Goal line: "Light the beacon" (so the winning move is to send him dark). Readout in truth-table terms, e.g. "In 1 lit → Lord Gainsay → Out 0 dark".
- **`map.js`:** exports `MAP`; `TILE` (1, centre to centre), `TILE_HEIGHT` (0.34), `TILE_TOP` (0, grass level), `ROAD_Y` (0.027, road surface); `tileCenter(row, col, y = ROAD_Y)` → `Vector3`. Axes: +x = map column (east), +z = map row (south), board centred on the origin. `path`: 17 shared `Vector3`s (clone before changing) on the road surface, S to B, with matching `pathTiles` `{row, col}`. `startTile`, `lordTile`, `beaconTile`: `{row, col, index, position}`, path index S = 0, N = 7, B = 16; N at (-1.5, ROAD_Y, -1), B at (0.5, ROAD_Y, 4). `lordFreeSide` = (0, 0, 1) (south). `lordSpot` = (-1.26, 0, -0.69), where the lord stands on the grass south of the outgoing road. `VIEW_SIDE` (the camera's side, used for the start hut and a straight N) = (0, 0, 1). The start hut sits on the east side of S, door to the road. `buildBoard()` → group `'board'` with children `tiles`, `road`, `startHut`, `gatehouse`, `beaconPlaceholder`, `decor`.
  - N is a bend (road in from the north, out to the east). The gatehouse fills the inside of the bend, and a stone arch spans the incoming north leg with its lintel underside about **0.48 above the grass**, so Hob must be shorter than that.
  - The camera looks from the south, so the lord stands in front of Hob at N and is seen from behind (black cloak and ruff; his face and red lining don't show). Hob, his torch and both gestures stay visible. The camera refits to every mesh in `board`, so **add moving pieces and their lights to `scene`, not `board`**. The `beaconPlaceholder` group sits at the B tile centre, with its tower 0.26 west of the road's end; `path`'s last point is the foot of the tower.
  - The moon is at (-6, 10, 4) (west-south-west), so the tiles' south sides read as dark earth; shadow camera ±9 covers the board.
- **`main.js`:** no static imports; loads three, `./map.js`, `./pieces.js` and `./run.js` with `Promise.all([import(...)])` inside `start()` and shows a plain message if that fails or WebGL is missing. `onFrame(fn)` calls `fn(dt, elapsed)` (seconds, `dt` capped at 0.1) each frame and returns an unsubscribe function; a throwing hook is logged once and the loop continues. Pixel ratio is reapplied on resize. `fitCamera(w, h, inset)` fits the board into the stage minus the insets (from `stageInset()`: the stage's safe-area padding, plus how far `.controls` overlaps the stage when it isn't `position: static`), keeping the frustum the stage's shape; it ignores an overlay that would leave under 30% of the stage. One ResizeObserver watches `#stage` and `.controls`. On desktop the insets are 0. `window.__gates = { scene, camera, renderer, board, onFrame, pieces: { hob, lord, beacon }, run: { send, reset, state } }`.
  - Page ids: `#stage`, `#torch-lit` (checked by default) and `#torch-dark` (radios, `name="torch"`, values 1 and 0), `#send`, `#reset`, `#readout`, `#status` (`aria-live="polite"`). A `.btn:disabled` style exists. The controls sit in a `.bar` wrapper (`display: contents` on desktop); the goal line has a second copy in the bar, and each copy is `display: none` at the other size.
- **`pieces.js`:** exports `update(dt, elapsed)` (registered with `onFrame`) and `reducedMotion()` (checked live).
  - `createHob({ hood?, lit? })` → `{ group, body, torch, isLit, setLit(lit, { animate }), torchTip(target?) }`. Move `group`; bob `body` while walking. `torchTip()` is the world position of the flame centre (about 0.4 high). Height 0.42, fits under the arch. Starts on S (`path[0]`) facing `path[1]`.
  - `createLord()` → `{ group, snuff(target, opts), light(target, opts) }`. `target` is a world `Vector3` or a function returning one each frame (e.g. `() => hob.torchTip()`). `opts` is `{ onContact }` or the callback itself. `onContact` fires once when the torch should flip; **the caller flips the torch** in it. The Promise resolves when he is back at rest. Snuff: contact about 0.8 s, done 1.7 s. Light: contact about 1.0 s, done 1.6 s. Reduced motion: contact on the first frame, done about 0.45 s. Gestures queue. Stands at `lordSpot` facing N.
  - `createBeacon()` → `{ group, bounds, isLit, setLit(lit, { animate }), firePoint(target?) }`. `firePoint()` is the brazier's coals (about 0.95 high), out of Hob's reach from the road's end (torch tip about 0.4), so lighting it uses a sparks/flame hand-off from `hob.torchTip()` to `beacon.firePoint()`. Starts dark. The board's `beaconPlaceholder` is hidden; the camera fit includes `beacon.bounds`.
  - `setLit` always returns a Promise: animate fades over 0.5 s (0.2 s under reduced motion), otherwise instant. A newer call cancels a running fade. Point lights don't cast shadows and stay at intensity 0 when dark.
- **`run.js`:** `N = lordTile.index`, `SPEED` 1.9 tiles a second. A run takes about 13 s (about 1.3–1.5 s under reduced motion, with a 0.35 s pause at N). During a run the radios are `disabled` and Send is `aria-disabled` (kept focusable so keyboard focus isn't dropped; clicks are ignored). Reset cancels with a run token at any point; Send again resets first.
